Blood Donation Camp

Blood Donation Drive - A Humanitarian Initiative

Under the aegis of the Dipali Trivedi Charitable Trust, we are committed to improving everyone’s lives. There have been consistent efforts put in by the trust to elevate awareness of voluntary blood donation amongst people, and ample space is made available to the donors within the Little Angels Higher Secondary School campus itself so that they can come and donate with ease. The fact that voluntary blood donation is not only a humanitarian cause but also a growing need of today makes our efforts even stronger with each passing day.

Event To Spread Awareness About EYE Donation

25/10/2024

People with blindness also have the right to see this beautiful world, and we at Dipali Trivedi Charitable Trust make sure that this message is put across loud and clear with our push for eye donation. We frequently inspire everyone to fill out eye donation forms and be a part of a cause as beautiful as this.The Trust believes that every individual, including those with blindness, has the right to experience the world’s beauty through sight and works to make this a reality.

NGO

People For Animals

The Dipali Trivedi Charitable Trust is also a part of the People for Animals Foundation spearheaded by Smt. Maneka Gandhi, a former member of the Lok Sabha. This is the largest animal welfare NGO in India, which is dedicated to protecting as well as promoting the welfare of animals.

Dipali Trivedi Charitable Trust

We offer free education from our trust for 10 students

So as to help bright and deserving students from low-income families, Dipali Trivedi Charitable Trust offers a scholarship opportunity for 10 children from LKG to Class V so they receive free education till Class 12. It is our way of telling them that we care and want you to march forward.

Blanket Distribution

Dipali Trivedi Charitable Trust identified a problem as steep as the unbearable cold weather and how big an issue it can be for people who live on streets. To take care of them well, the trust takes the onus of distributing blankets to the needy in the wake of rising cold and helps them with as much comfort as possible.
